DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Capability without dependency.

01

Human purpose first

Systems exist to increase agency and possibility, not to turn people into components of an operating model.

02

Stewardship without control

Make decision rights, ownership and boundaries visible. Responsibility should come with appropriate authority.

03

Evidence over appearance

Distinguish what is designed, implemented, verified, adopted and transferred. A diagram is not proof of operation.

04

Adapt or retire

Prefer reversible experiments, clear failure signals and systems that can be changed when they stop serving people.
