02 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Architecture in service of business decisions.

Strong systems are understandable by the people responsible for them, with explicit decision rights and room to adapt.

01

Understand the actual system

Map information flows, incentives, exceptions, and the gap between documented and observed behavior.

02

Align authority with responsibility

Make the owner, approver, operator, and escalation path visible before the work becomes critical.

03

Make change observable

Test contracts across boundaries and distinguish implemented, deployed, verified, and adopted states.

04

Leave people more capable

Build clear interfaces, reusable practices, and recoverable systems others can operate without the architect.
